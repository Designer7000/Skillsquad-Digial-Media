Before You Ask

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope — a logo refresh and a full identity system are very different projects. (placeholder ranges) As a rough guide: focused brand identities typically start around $3k, custom websites around $5k, and content or campaign engagements are scoped individually.

You always receive a written quote before any work begins, and it stays fixed unless the scope itself changes.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Our process is built for remote collaboration across time zones — structured video calls, shared workspaces and clear milestones keep everything moving. If you're in the same city, even better: we meet in person where it counts.

How do we start?

Send a project inquiry with a few sentences about what you're building. We reply within one business day with questions or a first call. From there you get a written proposal with scope, timeline and fixed pricing — there's no obligation until you sign.

Who owns the final files?

You do. Once the final invoice is paid, all deliverables, source files and usage rights transfer to you in full. No lock-in, no licensing surprises.

Can you handle just one piece of the puzzle?

Absolutely. Need only a logo, a website, or a month of social content? We take on standalone pieces as happily as full-stack projects — and everything is built so future work can plug in cleanly whenever you're ready.
