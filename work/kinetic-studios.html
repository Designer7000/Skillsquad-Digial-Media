Case Study — Brand Identity · 2026

Kinetic Studios

Identity and digital presence for a creative production studio — a kinetic type system and a portfolio site where the work is the brand.

01

Overview

Kinetic is a creative production studio — film, post, motion — whose portfolio looked like everyone else's: a dark grid, a showreel, a contact form. The work was strong; the wrapper was forgettable. Skillsquad rebuilt the identity and the digital presence around a single conviction: for a studio, the brand is the frame, and the work is the picture.

The engagement covered a motion-ready identity, a kinetic type system, a portfolio website with case templates, showreel titles and a pitch deck. The studio's own site became the strongest piece of new business collateral they own.

02

The Challenge

Kinetic was winning work on reputation and losing it on first impressions. Prospects landed on a site that could have belonged to any studio in any city, scrolled a reel wall, and left with no sense of how Kinetic actually thinks.

The deeper issue was positioning: the old site sold outputs — reels, edits, deliverables — while clients buy process. The new presence had to prove craft before a single meeting: how the studio approaches problems, not just what it shipped.

03

Strategy

Strategy: work is the brand. We designed the identity to recede — a confident, quiet frame — so the studio's output carries the personality. Case studies lead with the problem and the thinking, not the trophy shot; the site is structured as proof of process, with every project telling its own short story.

A kinetic type system gives the frame its energy: headlines that shift, stretch and resolve across the site, turning even static pages into demonstrations of motion craft. The identity sells the studio's core skill simply by existing.

The best studio identity is a great frame — it makes the work look inevitable.
04

Creative Direction

Direction: a Swiss grid gone feral. Rigorous underlying structure with headlines that break it on purpose — kinetic typography that shifts on scroll, documentary studio photography, and an acid-lime charge used like a highlighter, never a flood.

References came from title sequences, editorial design and the studio's own cutting room: timecode, frame edges, the honest mess of production. The site feels like being inside a working studio rather than looking at a brochure about one.

05

Design System

  • Paper#F4F3EF

  • Acid — project tint#C9F24E
Aa.
  • Display — Space Grotesk Bold. Tight tracking, kinetic behaviours defined in code — shift, stretch, resolve.
  • Body — Inter. For case narratives and credits — the thinking must read as clearly as the titles move.
  • Motion rule — type moves with purpose: it demonstrates craft, never decorates the page.
  • Kinetic type rulesThree defined behaviours — shift, stretch, resolve — coded once and reused everywhere.
  • Grid systemA strict 12-column grid that headlines are allowed to break. The tension is the style.
  • Motion mark variantsThe identity ships as static, sting and loop variants — ready for every screen the studio touches.
  • Case templateA repeatable storytelling structure so new projects publish in hours, not weeks.
06

Final Execution

The studio launched with an identity that behaves like their work and a website that sells their process. The case template means every new project strengthens the system, and the pitch deck carries the same kinetic language into the room where decisions get made.

07

Results

Results are measured against client KPIs and published with permission. For Kinetic, that means qualified inbound inquiries, time on case pages and pitch-to-win conversion.

The presence was designed to do one job before any meeting happens: prove that this studio thinks in motion, works in systems, and finishes what it starts.
